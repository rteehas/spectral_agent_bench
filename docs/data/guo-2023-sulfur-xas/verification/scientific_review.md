# Scientific review of executed worked examples

Status: pass for the scoped illustrative worked examples. The independent execution, code inspection, result tables and figures support the conclusions below. This does not make the candidate estimator a required benchmark solution, and numerical verifier success alone remains insufficient.

Q1 reconstructs all 66 native material responses and tests relative alignment and population weights separately over three Gaussian resolutions. The shape discrepancies support a material-dependent sensitivity conclusion: median alignment error is 12.8% at 0.5 eV, with a 41.5% maximum in material 066; 1 eV reduces the median to 5.8% but leaves a 25.6% maximum. Equal weights give a zero median because many ratios are equal, while exceptions retain nonzero distortion. The revised prompt asks where simplification is least reliable. Preservation of pairwise material similarity rankings was not computed and is explicitly outside this pass. Experimental detectability and exact lifetime broadening are not claimed.

Q2 uses material-level resampling and within-material adjustment rather than treating 2681 sites as independent replicates. It provides multiple spectral features and Li cutoffs. All 18 partial slopes are positive, but the nominal onset and peak within-material intervals include zero. The positive centroid association persists across cutoffs. The report properly rejects a general red-shift interpretation under these definitions without assigning a causal shielding mechanism. The global linear coordination adjustment is an operational summary; nonlinear relationships, local motifs and interactions are not exhaustively tested. Finite windows, the distinction between a centroid and an absorption threshold, uneven structure sampling and bootstrap precision remain limitations.

Q3 tests every glass structure without training on any of its sites; the model consumes only normalized spectral intensities. It compares crystal-only, glass and hybrid training at two resolutions, and reports minority recall, confusion matrices and conditional material-bootstrap intervals. At 0.5 eV, balanced accuracies are 0.679, 0.871 and 0.896 respectively. Crystal-only P2 recall is 31.1%, versus 71.1% for grouped glass training, exposing incomplete transfer. The majority predictor has 97.0% ordinary accuracy but balanced accuracy 0.333. Point estimates retain the training-regime ordering at 1 eV; no statistically significant smoothing benefit is claimed. Related ancestry/composition may cross folds, minority labels cover few structures, library size and coverage differ, and intervals do not include model-fitting uncertainty. These are disclosed limits, not evidence for measured bulk-spectrum performance.
