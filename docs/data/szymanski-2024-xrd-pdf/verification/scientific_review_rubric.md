# Evaluator-only scientific review rubric

The automatic verifier checks source identities, declared partition disjointness, prediction coverage, label vocabulary, core metric arithmetic, and artifact presence. `integrity_passed` is **not** a scientific-correctness verdict. Its checks neither select an algorithm nor require matching illustrative candidate predictions. Evaluate the submitted code, execution traces, report, figures, and saved evidence before assigning the following score. A defensible negative result may earn full credit; agreement with a paper's conclusions is not a scoring criterion.

## Mandatory validity gate

Evidence of test-label use in fitting, tuning, deciding phase count, or choosing a reported window invalidates the affected scientific comparison. Fabricated spectra/results or an execution trace that fails to support the reported analysis also invalidate it. Record the evidence, do not infer misconduct from low accuracy or an unexpected result. Source metadata labels are readable for independent scoring; automatic checks cannot prove that the scientific separation was respected. Rerun the submission in a fresh solver bundle and trace data dependencies. If execution cannot be reproduced, report that limitation rather than silently accepting the numbers.

## Score (100 points)

| Dimension | Points | Full-credit evidence | Partial / no credit |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Research design and independence | 25 | The scientific claim, evaluation unit, choices needing tuning, and held-out evaluation are explicit. Compared representations see the same eligible specimens. Fitting/tuning decisions precede test evaluation. Related replicates and restricted phase coverage are acknowledged. Controls distinguish the proposed effect from plausible confounds. | Deduct for weak controls, unsupported representativeness, accidental dependency, or using the same held-out observations to select and assess choices. Apply the validity gate to proven leakage. |
| Physical and numerical validity | 20 | Axis units, wavelength, reciprocal-space conversion, transform convention, range, normalization, and grids are correctly implemented and justified where consequential. A virtual PDF is interpreted as a finite-range transform of these released intensity patterns, not as a fully corrected experimental pair distribution. Perturbations and handling of different scan grids support the stated physical claim. | Deduct for unexplained preprocessing that can create the claimed effect, unit errors, physically unsupported artifacts, or unverified transforms. Code and saved arrays must support figures. |
| Quantitative evidence and comparisons | 25 | Core metrics match independent recomputation. Comparisons include suitable uncertainty and error analysis. Class, composition, and abundance imbalance are handled or disclosed. Task-specific evidence below directly addresses the question. | Deduct for point estimates alone when uncertainty changes the conclusion, incomplete comparisons, plot-only claims, or failure to investigate relevant errors. Additional submitted metrics must be independently checked before credit. |
| Interpretation and limitations | 20 | Conclusions follow from the actual numerical evidence, including tradeoffs, failures, alternative explanations, and the domain covered by the data. Superior performance is claimed only at the scope supported by the evaluation. | Deduct for causal claims from confounded comparisons, extrapolation to unseen materials or instrument settings, or confident ranking unsupported by uncertainty. An honest inconclusive finding is valid. |
| Reproducible execution | 10 | Runnable source, dependencies, data references, seeds where relevant, and commands reproduce tables and figures. Declared splits match actual fitted data. Saved evidence traces examples to source IDs. Independent rerun discrepancies are explained. | Deduct for missing execution detail, inaccessible inputs, omitted randomness, or provenance gaps. Artifact existence alone earns no credit. |

Score each dimension from zero to its maximum with a short evidence-based rationale. Report the total, the validity-gate status, the concrete limitations, and any unverified claims. Do not replace this review with a single pass/fail accuracy threshold.

## Question-specific evidence required for full credit

- **Q1 — representation and complementary errors:** Evaluate both chemical systems, compare XRD, virtual PDF, and a justified joint approach on identical held-out specimens, analyze paired errors and uncertainty, and test whether a representation conclusion survives consequential modeling choices. Class-wise diagnostics should reveal where a global score masks failure. Do not infer general representation superiority from one inadequately tuned classifier.
- **Q2 — unknown-cardinality mixture recovery:** Recover constituent phase identities and infer phase count without reading target phase labels/counts during prediction or threshold selection. Evaluate full phase sets and partial recovery against source labels, include count errors, and inspect representative false inclusions and exclusions. Differences between two- and three-phase groups are observational: their composition distributions may differ. A known-K upper bound is a permissible labeled diagnostic, not the main result.
- **Q3 — robustness and distance support:** Apply justified added noise and smooth-background perturbations to multiple phase identities and repeated specimens. Analyze where useful signal and contamination occur in distance, compare meaningful alternative windows, and validate a window decision with actual held-out phase identification under clean and perturbed conditions. Plotting transform distortion alone does not answer the identification question. Trace at least selected evidence arrays and plotted curves back to raw sources; independently reimplement representative transforms and perturbations from the solver's stated convention. The finite-array automatic check does not do this.
- **Q4 — transfer and minor phases:** Use the full eligible simulation-derived phase/formula library, handle polymorph-to-formula scoring consistently, and assess all provided experimental targets without using their phase identities to restrict candidates. Evaluate secondary-phase recovery by nominal abundance along with false positives or set precision, compare representations and a justified combination, and inspect the simulation–experiment mismatch. Nominal weight fractions are not spectral coefficients or independently measured purity. Report detection claims as specific to the provided mixture/instrument set, with uncertainty and sparse-group limitations.

## Calibration and acceptance

The included workflow is one worked solution demonstrating feasibility; it is not a required algorithm, a ceiling on solution quality, or a numerical answer key. Experimental phase labels and source files are verification anchors. Novel methods, learner-chosen partitions, different legitimate transforms, and better or worse predictions are acceptable submissions when they satisfy the prompt and survive scientific review. Record core performance separately from the 100-point scientific score so execution integrity, predictive capability, and quality of reasoning are not conflated.
