**Scientific result and limitations.** The representation genuinely spans three scales: quadratic coefficients over 10-, 20- and 50-point windows produce 51 descriptors. Pointwise and multiscale models share training/validation/test records and comparable tuning for coordination, distance and Bader charge across all eight elements. Training-majority/median baselines show that both learn useful spectral information.

The multiscale representation does **not** establish an improvement over pointwise input in any of the 48 paired conditional 95% intervals. Three distance comparisons and one charge comparison favor pointwise input; every coordination contrast is inconclusive. This supports retained useful information with some representation-dependent losses, not formal equivalence. No practical equivalence margin was specified. All test coordination classes are represented: the minimum class-4/5/6 test counts are 27/47/54.

Held-out joint perturbations and repeated partitions provide evidence of predictive reliance beyond an importance ranking. The strongest descriptor-midpoint bin agrees between representations in **29/48** paired comparisons; their mean five-bin rank correlation is **0.483**. These observations directly caution against treating similar predictive scores as matching interpretations. Wide 50-point descriptors cover roughly half the energy range even when their midpoints fall within a one-fifth bin. Thus neither that bin nor the associated coefficient is a uniquely localized physical mechanism.

The report appropriately limits unseen-material claims to the identified `scrape` population. Unknown FEFF material identities, aliases and structural relatives cannot be resolved from these inputs. Two overlapping material holdouts provide sensitivity rather than independent replications. The 250-draw material bootstrap conditions on the fitted models and is not multiplicity-adjusted. Three shuffles per energy bin and one per degree family describe perturbation sensitivity; they are not material-population confidence intervals for a physical attribution. Correlated features, off-manifold perturbations, one tree family and one multiscale construction limit interpretive generality. These are stated limits of the claim, not reasons to discard an otherwise defensible mixed result.
